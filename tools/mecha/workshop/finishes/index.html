Finish in the launcher previews

Readability is never a premium upgrade. All three palettes, clear focus, strong text contrast and reduced-motion support are free in this proposal. Cosmetic finishes buy a material treatment, not power, better odds or a “rarity” label.

Small effects, honest boundaries

The 64-pixel canvas uses the current Courier, Survey and Yard sheets, assembled legs → body → arms → head. Surface masks leave the dark linework, face, signal lights and transparency intact. No smoothing, glow blur or full-body strobe.

The 10-credit figures are ideas for optional direct purchases, not live prices. Credits would be fictional, earned through play, non-purchasable and non-tradable. This bench has no wallet, random reward or save access.

Keyboard: Tab between controls, arrow keys in selectors and finish choices, Enter or Space on buttons. No external fonts, libraries or network services. Serve the worktree root on localhost to load its original art.
